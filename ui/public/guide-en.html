Language

Choose Français or English at the top of the app, including during setup. Your choice is saved in this browser. Switching languages preserves your drafts. Your CVs, messages, listings and documents stay in their original language; ask your AI assistant to translate content when needed.

Your AI, your sources

  1. Choose DeepSeek, Kimi, Claude or OpenAI.
  2. Enter your API key and a chat model.
  3. Test and save the connection. This short call may incur a charge.
  4. Choose your country, profession, platforms and work preferences. Save your Bright Data key separately from your AI key. France Travail is available for France.
  5. Describe your work and activate your workspace.

No searches run automatically after installation. Chat app subscriptions do not replace an API key.

LinkedIn and Indeed

Bright Data scrapers collect public listings for your selected country and profession: up to 20 listings per platform and profession. They do not use your LinkedIn or Indeed account. Enable the corresponding scrapers in your Bright Data account, then use the connection test. Results may incur Bright Data charges; the local limit counts calls, not currency. Coverage depends on the country and provider.

Set the language for advice and documents in your search criteria, independently from the interface language.

Everyday use

Start with your profile, then request a search in the conversation. Save useful leads and prepare a CV or cover letter from a listing. Use advanced search criteria to choose sources, and monitoring settings to set the schedule. Every selected AI provider must be configured.

Review and approve email drafts before sending. The AI has no email-sending tool.

What stays with you

Profiles, conversations, documents and keys are stored in your installation’s Docker volume. Keys are not encrypted on disk: keep backups private. The AI receives the necessary context, and search providers receive the search criteria.

Costs depend on your providers. The local Bright Data counter is not your credit balance.

Stop and restart

From the package directory: docker compose stop, then docker compose up -d. Your data is preserved. See the README for backup and restore instructions.
